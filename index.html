Prof Themba Mosia

Leader in Higher Education Governance and Student Development

About

Prof Themba Mosia serves as Vice-Principal: Student Life at the University of Pretoria. His portfolio encompasses student life, residence affairs and accommodation, TuksFM, student sport initiatives, and related student development activities.

Portfolio Responsibilities

  • Student Life
  • Residence Affairs and Accommodation
  • TuksFM Community Radio Station
  • TuksSport and High Performance Centre Activities

Professional Experience

With more than four decades of experience in higher education, Prof Mosia has held leadership positions including lecturer, head of department, registrar, dean, deputy vice-chancellor, vice-principal, and university administrator.

His work focuses on governance, transformation, quality assurance, and strategic leadership in higher education institutions.

Academic Background

Prof Mosia holds a PhD in Policy Studies with a focus on governance and transformation in higher education. He has also undertaken specialist training in quality assurance, medical education, management, law, and policy studies.

Leadership and Public Service

Prof Mosia has served in numerous national and international leadership roles, including chairing quality assurance and accreditation processes, contributing to higher education policy, and advising on governance and student development initiatives.
